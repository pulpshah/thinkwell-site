Trust the number

AI personas, checked by verified people every wave.

BuiltNo live panel yet. The first verified people are our own team.

Thinkwell’s AI personas predict how people would answer. A smaller group of verified people, picked at random, answers for real, and the two are combined so the result is sharper than the verified group alone, with error bars that hold for the sample you actually have.

Every new wave measures again how closely the personas track real people. Good personas make each verified person count for more, so the same confidence costs fewer people. Drifting personas widen the error bars instead of quietly making the number wrong.

Up to 35%less sampling noise in simulation, at zero bias, against a plain human-only estimate.
